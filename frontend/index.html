    How to use this page

    1. Pick a day in the calendar on the Explore tab. ★ marks an unseen day: the fair test.
    2. Read the implied volatility: what the options market expected, against our forecast and what happened.
    3. Click a time in the one-day chart. Steps 4 and 5 show the strategies and what each model said at that time.
    4. Read the verdict in step 6. "How it's built" shows the pipeline; "Controls" is for the operator.

    "Show the guided tour" on the Explore tab opens the short tour again.
